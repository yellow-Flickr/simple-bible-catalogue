# What's New

## 1.0.0

The first release of Simple Bible.

- **Reader:** read chapter by chapter, highlight and jump to any verse.
- **Versions:** switch translations from anywhere, and download more from the
  version library.
- **Topics:** explore Scripture by more than 160 topics.
- **Verse of the Day and daily reflections**, with a history you can filter
  by topic.
- **Search:** jump straight to a reference, or search words and phrases across
  the Bible, your notes and topics.
- **Notes, favourites and reading history.**
- **Light and dark themes.**
