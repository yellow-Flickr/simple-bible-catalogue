# Privacy Policy

**Simple Bible** by CodeKraft
Effective 12 November 2026

## Summary

Simple Bible keeps your data on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics,
no ads and no tracking, and it never sends your personal information to us or
to anyone else.

## What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following **on your device only**:

- your display name and when you started using the app
- your preferred Bible version and theme
- your notes, favourite verses and reading history
- the daily reflections you have been shown
- your recent searches
- the Bible translations you have downloaded

We cannot see any of this.

## Internet access

The app uses the internet for one thing only: **getting Bible translations**.

- The list of available translations is fetched from GitHub
  (`raw.githubusercontent.com`).
- Translations you choose to download come from the jsDelivr CDN
  (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`).

These requests contain nothing about you. Like any website, those services
can see your device's IP address when they deliver a file, and they handle it
under their own privacy policies:
[GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
and [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).

Everything else (reading, searching, topics, reflections and notes) works
offline.

## Other apps

When you tap **Rate & review**, **Send feedback**, **Support us**, **Share** or
any other link, the app hands over to another app or website (the Play Store,
your email app, Buy Me a Coffee, your browser or the share sheet). That app's or
site's own privacy policy then applies.

## Feedback

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use
it only to reply and to improve the app. We do not sell or share it.

## Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, permanently deletes
everything listed above.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
